# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Crew booth**: freelancer or event staff, newly trained, not technical about cameras, printers, or software. They set up the booth at a venue (pair, pick event, check camera/printer), then watch it during a 3–8 hour event, fixing paper, failed prints, or a disconnected camera under time pressure and noisy, crowded conditions. They need step-by-step guidance in everyday Indonesian, never jargon.
- **Guests**: wedding/corporate event attendees or mall walk-ins using a 1920×1080 touchscreen kiosk, standing, often in groups, often in dim or mixed venue lighting. They have seconds of attention and zero training: one obvious action per screen.
- **Guests on phone**: open the QR page (390 px mobile web) to view and save strip, originals, GIF, and video.
- **Owner/admin (Tetra Photobooth)**: creates events, templates, prices, and booth assignments in the web admin on a laptop.

## Product Purpose

Tetra Booth replaces LumaBooth/dslrBooth for Tetra Photobooth (Indonesia): an offline-first Windows kiosk (event mode = free prints, photobox mode = QRIS payment), a cloud admin, and guest pages. Success = a crew member with one briefing can run an event alone, guests finish a session without asking for help, and every photo reaches the guest's phone.

## Brand Personality

Playful, warm, trustworthy. Pastel fills with firm ink outlines and offset "layered" cards (design v2). The guest's photos and the event's branding are the hero; the UI frames them. Crew screens are the same family but calmer and more utilitarian.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS dashboards: hero-metric cards, identical icon grids, gradient accents, glassmorphism.
- dslrBooth/LumaBooth settings screens: dense technical forms, jargon ("EDSDK", "Camera Service"), tiny controls.
- Text crammed against button or card borders; flat grids of equally weighted buttons with no order.

## Design Principles

1. **Function first, then beauty.** Usability decides layout; aesthetics polish it, never override it.
2. **One obvious next step.** Every screen has a single primary action; crew tasks follow an explicit order.
3. **Speak crew language.** Everyday Indonesian, say what to do and what happens next; no technical terms in the primary path.
4. **Built for a noisy venue.** Big touch targets, generous padding, readable at arm's length, recoverable errors.
5. **Photos are the hero.** UI chrome stays quiet around guest photos and event branding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Touch kiosk: targets ≥ 64 px on booth, ≥ 44 px on mobile; text ≥ 18 px on booth.
- Contrast WCAG AA; text is always ink on pastel (never pastel text).
- Respect reduced motion; no information conveyed by color alone (status uses text + icon).
- All UI copy in Indonesian.
